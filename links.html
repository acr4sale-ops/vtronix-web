Resources

Standards and listings that matter to a control buyer.

The marks and approvals you will see referenced across our product pages, and what each one actually covers.

UL

Underwriters Laboratories. Independent product-safety testing and certification, widely required by US electrical inspectors and code officials.

CSA

CSA Group. Canada's equivalent of UL certification for equipment sold into the Canadian market.

ETL

Intertek's ETL mark. A nationally recognized testing laboratory (NRTL) certification accepted anywhere UL is.

FCC

Federal Communications Commission. Certifies that a device will not cause harmful radio interference, required for anything with a microcontroller or radio sold in the US.

CE

Conformite Europeenne. A manufacturer's declaration that a product meets EU health, safety and environmental requirements, required to sell into the European Economic Area.

AHRI

Air-Conditioning, Heating and Refrigeration Institute. Its certification programs verify the published performance ratings of HVAC equipment.

ENERGY STAR

The EPA and DOE's voluntary program labeling appliances and equipment that meet defined energy-efficiency thresholds.

ASHRAE

American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers. Publishes the technical standards most HVAC equipment and building codes are written around.
