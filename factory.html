Next Gen Boards

When an off-the-shelf product does not fit the application, we can help find a solution for YOU.

We have world-class individuals and facilities ready to help you compete in the global market. We are seasoned professionals with demonstrated experience in all areas of electronic control design and manufacturing.

1

Application

We start by understanding your application. We work with you to understand the environment and other idiosyncrasies involved with creating your successful product.

2

Specification

Once an application is understood, an engineering specification for the product is developed and approved. Often the specification will be iterated with engineering and factory personnel to achieve the best cost performance tradeoffs for your product and application.

3

Design and Test

Prior to production, products are tested to assure compliance with the approved development specification. These tests include product testing in the factory and application testing in the United States. If listing agency approvals (such as UL, CSA, or ETL) or government approvals (such as FCC and CE) are required, Vtronix will complete the required tests and approvals.

4

Production

Product will be produced under a Rigorous Quality control system in our State-of-the-Art production facility. Products are inventoried and distributed from our warehouse in Miami Gardens, Florida. We carry product liability insurance for the benefit of our customers.

5

Quality Control

To detect problems quickly, returns are first analyzed locally. Regular reviews are held with our factory to maintain high quality levels.
