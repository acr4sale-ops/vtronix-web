Air Conditioning Control

R100A

R100A A/C Control Board. HP & LP switch inputs.

Documentation

Download

The R100A sheet describes an electronic integrated air-conditioner control for blower and compressor operation. Features printed on it are LED status lights, freeze protection, high-pressure and low-pressure safety cutout, an adjustable delay on make of 0.03 to 10 minutes, a 3 minute delay on break for compressor protection, an adjustable 10 to 90 second post purge, lockout mode, test mode, and an alarm relay. The sheet says OEM custom modifications are available.

Ratings are 18 to 30 Vac and -40 C to 75 C. The blower relay is SPDT, 50 LRA / 16 FLA at 120 Vac. The compressor relay is SPST, 24 Vac, 1 amp. The alarm relay is DPDT, 24 Vac, 1 amp. PCB material is CEM1. A green LED is power. The red LED is 1 blink if HPC has opened twice, 2 blinks if LPC has opened twice, and 3 blinks for freeze-stat lockout. Inputs shown are thermostat G, Y, and R, a freeze thermistor, and LPS and HPS.
