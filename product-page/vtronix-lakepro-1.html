LAKEPRO-1

Wifi Enabled, Programmable Thermostat. (Requires a C Wire)

Documentation

English Manual Spanish Manual

The English manual calls LAKEPRO-1 a Wi-Fi touchscreen programmable thermostat and an easy-to-use 7-day programmable thermostat for gas, oil, and electric conventional systems and heat pumps, up to 3 heat stages and 2 cool stages. It says the thermostat can remotely monitor and control heating and cooling. The manual's address block is 1001 Park Centre Blvd, Miami Gardens, Florida, and 305.471.7600. This page says the thermostat is Wi-Fi enabled and programmable and that it requires a C wire.

Features in the manual include a large clear backlit display of room temperature, set temperature, and humidity; menu-driven programming; capacitance touchscreen interaction; and an optional internet connection to monitor and control the system. The contents list application, features, specifications, installation, wiring, power-up, installer setup, operation, schedule programming, an FCC note, and troubleshooting. Spanish and English manuals are both linked on this page.
