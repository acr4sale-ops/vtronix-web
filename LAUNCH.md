# Vtronix V2 launch checklist

1. Lower web-record DNS TTL one day before cutover. Leave MX and all mail records unchanged.
2. Attach both `vtronix.com` and `www.vtronix.com` to the Vercel project, set `www` as the primary domain, and confirm the apex → `www` redirect in `vercel.json` fires (it depends on both domains being attached; it could not be exercised in local dev).
3. Verify SSL, then run `python3 scripts/check-urls.py https://www.vtronix.com vtronix-url-map.json` (point it at the merged/complete map — see `URL-REPORT.md`) and confirm every URL is a 200 or a single 301 to a 200. As of the last preview, 118/118 mapped URLs are single-hop 200s except 2 legacy `/products.html?cat=` query links that were never real Wix URLs (see `URL-REPORT.md` for why, and confirm they still don't matter before launch). Also run `node scripts/crawl-check.mjs https://www.vtronix.com` for a broader internal-link sweep beyond just the Wix map.
4. Confirm `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, canonical tags and analytics are production-ready. `sitemap.xml` regenerates on every Vercel build (see `buildCommand` in `vercel.json`) and now includes every page with real content — home, factory, about, links, contact, custom-controls, capabilities, brands, applications + its 6 sub-pages, documentation, request-a-quote, every category and every product. Certifications and the 4 legal pages are deliberately excluded (and `noindex`ed) until their `[CONFIRM]` placeholders are replaced with real text — remove the `noindex` meta tag from each and add it to `generate-sitemap.py`'s `STATIC_PAGES` once that happens.
5. Confirm Vercel's default preview-deployment `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` header is present on the preview URL (no custom middleware was added for this — Vercel sends it automatically) and confirm production does not have it.
6. Before launch, grep the built output for every `[CONFIRM` and cross-check against `CONFIRM.md` — nothing on that list should reach production. Certifications and the 4 legal pages are the biggest blockers (they're currently 100% placeholder).
7. GA4 is not wired up yet — needs a real property ID from the owner, then the events the brief calls for (quote submitted, manual downloaded, phone click, brand link click) need adding.
8. Submit the production sitemap through Google Search Console.
